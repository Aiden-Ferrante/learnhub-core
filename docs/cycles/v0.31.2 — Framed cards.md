# v0.31.2 — Framed cards

Light cycle under [[0.) Development Protocol 1.0]]
Opened 2026-11-12

Addresses **Make learning more efficient**

## Problem

After v0.31.1 the human liked the cover cards but asked (2026-11-12) for a border around each
card again and for the PDF image to be about 30% smaller. Opened although 'Competency' holds the
focus, on the standing go-ahead for the redesign cycles.

## Appetite

Under an hour.

## Plan

1. `course-card.tsx`: the whole card is a bordered `bg-card` panel with padding; the border
   lightens on hover.
2. The cover sits centred at 76% of the padded card width (about 70% of the old cover).

**Non-goals.** Changing the grid, the colours or the card's contents.

## Exit criteria

- On the Library, every course is a framed card with a smaller cover inside.
- Nothing scrolls sideways at 375px.

## Build

- One file: `surfaces/library/course-card.tsx`. The cover's own border stays, on `bg-background`.

## Test

- `tsc`, `eslint` and `check-hubs` are clean.
- Live at 1483px (`assets/v0.31.2/library.jpg`): 5 framed cards per row, covers centred inside,
  due badge and studied edge intact.
- 375px same-origin iframe: 363/363.

## Review

**What did the plan miss?** The narrower text column truncates long titles sooner
("A Philosoph…"). Acceptable for now; the full title is on hover through the course page.

**What did the agent need that the prompt did not give?** Nothing.

**What did I learn?** The frame gives the card back its edge without the cover having to carry it.

Decision: ship.

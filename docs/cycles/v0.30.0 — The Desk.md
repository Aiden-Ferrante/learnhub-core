# v0.30.0 — The Desk

Sub-cycle plan under [[0.) Development Protocol 1.0]]
Opened 2026-11-09

Addresses **Make learning more efficient**

> Each of fidelity, retention, competency and engagement carries evidence from one real book I studied end to end — not a demo corpus, and not the same book for all four

## Problem

A course is split across pages that do not know about each other: course home, reader, review,
packs and concepts. Each is two or three clicks apart through breadcrumbs, and moving between
them loses the book. The human felt this before they could name it. Shown three layouts on
2026-11-09, they chose "The Desk", one workspace per course, and said to go ahead.

Opened although 'Competency' holds the focus, on the same go-ahead as v0.29.0.

## Appetite

One session of agent time.

## Plan

1. `CourseDesk` (surfaces/library) wraps every course route through
   `app/courses/[courseId]/layout.tsx`.
2. **Mode bar:** Overview · Read (to the last reading position) · Review (with due count) ·
   Packs · Concepts. The current mode is marked.
3. **Chapter rail** (lg and up): each top-level section with its studied share
   (`coverage` from the mastery rollup), a memory-state dot, and the chapter being read marked.
   The rail sticks under the header and scrolls on its own.
4. The pages inside stay as they are.

**Non-goals.**
- The mockup's right column (section concepts, card history, test skills).
- Nesting sections inside the rail.
- Redesigning the review card.
- "Test" as its own mode: the mockup's Practice and Test are the existing Packs page, with its
  Learn and Exam tabs.

## Exit criteria

- On :3001, the Overview, Read, Review, Packs and Concepts pages of a real course all show the
  same bar and rail.
- The rail's numbers are the mastery API's.
- No page scrolls sideways at 375px.
- The fleet check is clean.

## Build

- `course-desk.tsx`: the mode comes from the pathname, and the current chapter is found by
  walking the tree for the reader's scope. The reader keeps its scope in the address bar through
  `replaceState`, and Next's `usePathname` follows that.
- The mode is labelled "Packs", not the mockup's "Practice / Test". That is the honest name for
  what the page is. Recorded as a deviation from the mockup.
- The rail shows *studied* (reviewed / total), not "known". Retention is shown only as the dot.
  "Known" would claim more than the data says.
- **Found in verify:** the header was centred at 72rem while the Desk is full-bleed, so the
  brand and the course title sat 160px apart. The header is now full width.
- **Found in verify:** the course home repeated the course title right under the Desk bar. Its
  heading is now "Overview".
- **Found in verify:** at 375px the course home scrolled sideways (398/375). The outline card's
  "Read the book · Default · Edit material" row was `shrink-0`. Sheet header actions now wrap.

## Review

**What did the plan miss?** That a frame changes how the pages inside read. The duplicated
title and the misaligned header only showed once the Desk was around them. The reader now has
two left rails, one for chapters and one for this chapter's headings. That works, but it is the
first thing to fold together.

**What did the agent need that the prompt did not give?** Nothing new. The mockup was enough to
build against, and its gaps (no "Test" page, no per-chapter "known") were settled in favour of
what the data supports.

**What did I learn?** A layout file is the cheapest place to fix fragmentation: one wrapper, no
page rewritten. The expensive half of the Desk is the right column, which needs per-section
context from three different surfaces. It is the next cycle if the human wants it.

Decision: ship.

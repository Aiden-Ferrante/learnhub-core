# v0.31.0 — Bookshelf

Sub-cycle plan under [[0.) Development Protocol 1.0]]
Opened 2026-11-11

Addresses **Make learning more efficient**

> Each of fidelity, retention, competency and engagement carries evidence from one real book I studied end to end — not a demo corpus, and not the same book for all four

## Problem

The human found Study Hall hard to read: its warm brown ground and cream ink were a low-contrast
orange cast. They asked on 2026-11-10 for "a dark black, not an orange hue". The Library was still
the fleet-era grid of identical grey cards, and the human said they had liked it when each course
had its own colour. Of two Library mockups on the canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/E4AQgKt4SmVo8TUspL2iA1,
page "Library"), they chose **2 · Bookshelf**. They also asked that its Review button open a page
like the top of **1 · Next Up**, the ordered session.

Opened although 'Competency' holds the focus, on the same standing go-ahead as v0.29.0 and v0.30.0.

## Appetite

One session of agent time.

## Plan

1. **Palette.** Neutral black ground (`0 0% 5%`), grey cards and borders, near-white ink. Terracotta
   stays as the one colour that means "act here".
2. **Course colours.** `coverColor(title)` picks one of six muted hues. None of them is a
   memory-state colour or the accent.
3. **Bookshelf.** Each course card is a cover: a wash of its colour, initials, due pill, serif title,
   then author, a studied bar in its colour, and Continue.
4. **Today strip.** One row across the top: what is due, a dot for each course, and one primary
   button ("Review N", or "Up next" when nothing is due). The button opens `/session`.
5. **Up next (`/session`).** Reviews (most due first), then each course's active pack step, then up
   to three reading positions. Each step is numbered and striped in its course's colour, with the
   first marked, and "Start session" opens step 1.
6. **Desk.** The course title gets its colour dot.

**Non-goals.** Time estimates for reviews and reading, since only pack steps carry minutes. A
cross-course review queue: Start opens the first course's review. Changing the group sidebar.

## Exit criteria

- On :3001: the Library shows coloured covers and the Today strip, and Review opens /session with
  real steps.
- Library, /session and a course page don't scroll sideways at 375px.
- The fleet check is clean.

## Build

- `globals.css`: neutral values under the same token names. The old `.today-list` rule is gone with
  the Today panel (`today-panel.tsx` was deleted, and `up-next.tsx` replaces it).
- `up-next.tsx`: `useUpNext()` builds the steps. Reviews come from the study-debt read and reading
  from saved positions. Pack steps come from one invisible `PackProbe` per course, because hooks
  cannot loop; each probe reports its step up.
- **Found in verify, fixed:** all 19 covers fetched mastery at once. The API's pool of 8 database
  connections hit `PoolTimeout` and study-debt took 37 s, so the strip hung on "Checking what is
  due…". Covers now fetch only when scrolled into view (IntersectionObserver, 200px margin).
  Afterwards study-debt took 3 ms at idle.
- **Found in verify, fixed:** the covers' colour was too faint on black (alpha 0x38 → 0x66). Books
  with no concepts showed "—" and now say "No concepts yet". Strip labels were cut off at 40
  characters, and pack labels repeated the course name.
- With six hues, two courses can share a colour (ML and Python Essentials 1 both drew plum). That
  is accepted, because the title always sits beside the colour.

## Review

**What did the plan miss?** That a picture per card costs a request per card. The old shelf made one
read for the whole library. The cover's studied bar made 19, and that was enough to starve the pool
of 8. A shelf that grows needs a batch endpoint for per-course mastery, or the lazy load holds.

**What did the agent need that the prompt did not give?** Nothing. The canvas mockups settled the
look before any code was written, and that was worth the extra round.

**What did I learn?** "Hard to read" on a warm palette was mostly hue, not contrast: the ratios
passed, but the cast tired the eye. Neutral black with one warm accent reads calmer.

Decision: ship.

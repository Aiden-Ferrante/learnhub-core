## Problem

Review does not make sense today. The 2026-11-21 audit found five entry points over three systems:
the course review page (due only), the reader's review drawer (due + every new concept, up to 40),
the path's Check understanding (due + every new concept in a section: 29 in CSE-5709 6.3), Up next,
and exam-pack skills. One reveal-and-grade flashcard serves all of them; the hub card's count
disagreed with the session it opened (1 vs 2); the structure card's answer was a July snapshot of
2 of 5 sections. The human stripped the hub Review card (`d65c251`) to start fresh.

The flow the human wants (2026-11-21): **read** in the guided reader → **review** the concepts in an
environment that helps learn them, "think Quizlet" → **exam** with a dynamic generator.

## Appetite

Two sessions. Scope bends, the timebox does not.

## Plan

Human decisions, 2026-11-21: Learn mode first · in-session mastery **and** spaced repetition after
· per section · exam always open · layout **B** (question left, section rail right) · typed answers
are **explanations in sentences, not one-word terms**, and the non-multiple-choice parts are
**mostly self-graded**. Design: canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/EozjHxZWJACtT9qtrUByQT, board
"B · Learn session v2" (playable).

Rungs:
1. **Walking skeleton** — route `/courses/[id]/chapters/[key]/learn/[n]` (section n of the path):
   loads the section's concepts, layout B, one multiple-choice question, rail, no persistence.
2. **Pick the definition** — term + 4 definitions; wrong options are other concepts' definitions
   from the same chapter (never outside the book); a miss says whose definition was picked and
   re-queues. Right → Learning.
3. **Explain it** — textarea → "Compare with the book" (or "Don't know, show me") → your answer
   beside the concept's definition and the book's sentence → self-grade Missed it / Partly / Got it.
   Got it → Mastered; Missed / Partly re-queue before the section ends. Keys 1–3.
4. **Persist to spaced repetition** — at the end, each concept's first explain grade seeds or
   advances its card through the existing `/api/review/grade` (Missed → Again, Partly → Hard,
   Got it → Good). No new table.
5. **End of section** — mastered / missed list, when they come back, "Next: read <next section>".
6. **Entry points** — the path's per-section action "Check understanding" becomes **Learn** (after
   reading); the hub Review card gets its states: learn the section just read · N due · all caught
   up · opens after your first section. Due cards run the same screen, explain-only.

**Non-goals.** Key-point chips (see D1). Typed-answer auto-grading. Match / flashcard modes. The
course-wide review page, the reader drawer and pack skills stay as they are. Structure and chapter
cards are not shown in Learn. Real exams.

**Decision points** (human):
- D1 key points on the reveal: generated per concept and grounded, or none in v0.32.0?
- D2 where Learn opens: its own page (as designed) vs inside the step reader after the last part.
- D3 in-session "Learning" state is browser-only (Mastered is the server's card); OK?

## Exit criteria

- PoSD ch 2 on :3001 in Chrome: 2.2 opens Learn with its 4 concepts; a wrong pick names whose
  definition it was and returns; an explanation compares and self-grades; the end screen lists all 4.
- After the session, the course review queue holds those 4 concepts with due dates matching their
  grades (observed in `/api/review/queue`).
- Hub Review card on PoSD ch 2 shows the right state and its count equals what the session serves.
- 375px: no sideways scroll on Learn and the hub.
- The human plays a section end to end and decides.

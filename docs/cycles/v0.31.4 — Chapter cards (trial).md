## Problem

On 2026-11-12 the human settled an Overview direction on the design canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/E4AQgKt4SmVo8TUspL2iA1, page Overview, boards "Overview D · Cards only"
and "Chapter dashboard"): "I want the cards to be the only thing on the screen % studied and
notifcations like a phone app that signals things to review. Then when you click on a card it
opens up to that chapters own dashboard." It was never built or recorded. On 2026-11-19: "Lets do
this design, but as a mock. I don't know if I will like it. So lets build it out and then I will
test to see what works and what doesn't."

## Appetite

An afternoon.

## Plan

1. `/courses/[id]/chapters`: one card per top-level chapter (number, title, % studied, bar in the
   course colour, red due badge). Due counts from ONE whole-course review queue, bucketed by
   chapter through the tree.
2. `/courses/[id]/chapters/[key]`: the chapter's dashboard — back link, number, title, studied
   line and bar; Read (continue or start), Review (due badge, opens the reader's review drawer on
   this chapter), Practice (the course's packs); then its sections with % and NEXT.
3. A "Try chapter cards" link on the current Overview; a "back to the current Overview" link on
   both trial pages.

**Non-goals.** Replacing the current Overview. Per-chapter practice (packs are course-wide today).
Moving Progress or Study activity. The mode bar.

## Exit criteria

- On PE1 and CSE-5709 in the human's Chrome: cards render with real numbers, studied shares and
  badges that match the course's due count; a card opens its dashboard; every action lands on a
  working page; no sideways scroll at 375px.
- The human tries it and says what works and what doesn't.

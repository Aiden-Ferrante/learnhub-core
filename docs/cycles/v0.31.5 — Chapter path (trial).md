## Problem

The v0.31.4 chapter hub is three equal cards (Read, Review, Exam) over a section list: nothing
says what to do next, cards are mostly empty, studied share appears three times, the section list
is noisy, concepts are buried. The human, 2026-11-19: "The exam should be the desition, we should
break down a chapter into to make it easy to digest, and we want the reading to be guided and very
clean. The reading is what leads to concept understanding which then leads to exams."

## Appetite

An afternoon.

## Plan

A trial route `/courses/[id]/chapters/[key]/path` beside the current hub ("Try the path layout"):
steps = the chapter's sections that carry concepts; each step Read → Check understanding; a step
is understood at 80% of its concepts studied; only the current step open (what you will learn +
one primary button); due reviews on their step; concept-less sections as Extras; the chapter exam
as the destination, always takeable, showing readiness; one progress line; no chapter rail.

**Non-goals.** Replacing the current hub. Real exams (v0.32.0). Changing the reader.

## Exit criteria

- On CSE-5709 chapter 6 in the human's Chrome: steps, current step, due badge on its step, exam
  at the end; every link lands; no sideways scroll at 375px.
- The human tries it and decides.

# v0.31.1 — Book covers

Light cycle under [[0.) Development Protocol 1.0]]
Opened 2026-11-11

Addresses **Make learning more efficient**

> Each of fidelity, retention, competency and engagement carries evidence from one real book I studied end to end — not a demo corpus, and not the same book for all four

## Problem

The human's feedback on v0.31.0: the course cards "look a bit ugly". They wanted the colours to
fit the black theme better, smaller cards so more show at once, and the PDF's own cover on each
card.

## Appetite

Under one session.

## Plan

1. `GET /api/document/cover?doc=`: page 1 of the original PDF as a 360px-wide JPEG, rendered with
   PyMuPDF once and cached as `cover.jpg` beside it. 404 when there is no PDF. Proxied at
   `/api/learn/cover`.
2. The card becomes a book: a 3:4 cover (the PDF page, or a quiet tile in the course colour), a due
   badge, studied share as the cover's bottom edge, and Continue on hover. Underneath sit a colour
   dot, the title and "N% studied". The grid is `minmax(140px,1fr)`.
3. The six course hues are softened to sit on the black.

**Non-goals.** Covers for EPUB or markdown books (they get the colour tile). Choosing a cover by
hand.

## Exit criteria

- On :3001, PDF books show their own cover and the others show the tile.
- At 1536px, 5 or more covers fit in a row, and at 375px nothing scrolls sideways.
- The due counts load without starving the pool.

## Build

- Grid: 5 per row next to the group sidebar at 1536px (3 before), and 2 per row at 375px.
- **Found in verify, fixed:** smaller cards put about 15 on screen at once, so v0.31.0's
  load-on-scroll still fired about 15 `/api/mastery` reads together. The pool hit `PoolTimeout`
  again. Added `GET /api/mastery/overall?docs=a,b,…`, which computes book after book on one
  connection. The shelf now makes one read (2 books in 87 ms) instead of 19.
- Right after the API restarts, study-debt takes about 37 s cold; warm, it takes 2 ms. That is
  not new and is not this cycle's work. It is carried to the Backlog.

## Test

- `tsc`, `eslint` and `check-hubs` are clean. The cover endpoint gave 200 image/jpeg, 59 KB in
  0.21 s the first time; cached, it took 3 ms. Without a PDF it gave 404 and the tile showed.
- **Live, Chrome at 1536px** (`assets/v0.31.1/library.jpg`, `library-scrolled.jpg`): the real
  covers show for A Philosophy of Software Design, AI Engineering, Fluent Python, Learning SQL,
  the Python Language Reference, Linear Algebra Done Right, Mathematics for ML, Designing
  Data-Intensive Applications and Deep Learning with PyTorch. Markdown courses show tiles. Under
  the covers: "7% studied", "0% studied", "No concepts yet". Hovering APoSD shows Continue and the
  "2 due" badge.
- **375px:** 363/363 with two 148px columns.
- `fleet-ui/check.sh`: clean.

## Review

**What did the plan miss?** That a smaller card multiplies whatever each card fetches. The batch
read is the real fix, and v0.31.0's lazy load was only a patch.

**What did I learn?** Real covers beat generated ones. The colour now works best as a small dot
and an edge, not as the whole card.

Decision: ship.
